Privacy Policy for Fixtura

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Fixtura ("the app") is an offline tournament generator, bracket manager, and team roulette drafting application developed by Louay Barraq. This policy explains what information the app handles and how your data is protected.

Data We Do Not Collect

Fixtura does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device identifiers. The app functions completely offline and has no user accounts, no sign-in requirements, and no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All data created in the app is stored exclusively on your device using local SQLite database storage (sqflite) and standard local preferences (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any external server or third party:

  • Tournament details (names, formats, legs, and custom point systems)
  • Participant names and team names
  • Match schedules, fixtures, results, and entered scores
  • Roulette options and drafting assignments
  • Onboarding completion status

This data is stored solely on your local device and is completely erased if you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Network & Internet Access

Fixtura operates 100% offline. The application does not require or make any network requests, API calls, or server connections.

Permissions

Fixtura does not request any sensitive Android device permissions (such as Location, Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or External Storage).

Children's Privacy

Fixtura does not collect any personal data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated in the future, this page will be revised and the "Last updated" date will reflect the changes.

Contact

If you have questions or feedback about this privacy policy, you can reach out via the GitHub Issues page.
